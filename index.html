Courses

1-on-1 Nail Course

My courses are individual, one-on-one training sessions, designed to give you my full attention and a personalized learning experience.

The course takes place over one full day and lasts approximately seven to eight hours. During the training, you will work on one or two models, depending on the course program and your learning pace.

We will combine theory with hands-on practice, so you can understand every step of the process, improve your technique and feel more confident in your work.
